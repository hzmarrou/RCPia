# RCPia Clinical Nutrition Source Report

## Bottom line

No major body publishes a standalone clinical nutrition guideline specifically for prostate cancer or bladder/urothelial cancer.

RCPia should therefore use a layered evidence model:

1. **ESPEN Clinical Nutrition in Cancer 2021** as the main nutrition authority.
2. **EAU prostate or bladder guideline** for disease- and treatment-specific context.
3. **ESPEN Clinical Nutrition in Surgery 2021** for radical cystectomy and prostatectomy.
4. **ESMO Cachexia 2021** for advanced disease/cachexia.
5. **ERAS cystectomy guidance** for bladder-specific peri-operative details, clearly flagged as dated.
6. French AFU/SFNCM sources where French clinical practice is relevant.

## Prostate-specific sources

### EAU-EANM-ESTRO-ESUR-ISUP-SIOG Guidelines on Prostate Cancer

- **Organisation:** EAU and partner societies
- **Version:** 2026
- **Scope:** Prostate-specific
- **Clinical use:** Frailty and health screening, CGA referral, nutritional status, treatment fitness, ADT-related bone care
- **Type:** Clinical practice guideline
- **Official URL:** https://uroweb.org/guidelines/prostate-cancer
- **Access/language:** Open HTML; English
- **Useful content:**
  - G8 for patients over 70; `<=14` prompts specialist geriatric assessment.
  - CGA explicitly includes nutritional status.
  - Three-month weight loss: `<5%` good status, `5-10%` risk, `>10%` severe malnutrition.
  - Calcium/vitamin D guidance in defined bone-protective treatment contexts.
- **Limitations:** Nutrition content is brief; no complete dietitian, EN/PN or protein/energy pathway.
- **Decision:** **Primary RAG** for prostate context. G8 arithmetic belongs in the deterministic tool layer.

### Updated SIOG Recommendations on Prostate Cancer Management in Older Patients

- **Organisation:** International Society of Geriatric Oncology
- **Year:** 2019
- **Scope:** Older adults with prostate cancer
- **Clinical use:** G8, CGA, nutrition/frailty and treatment adaptation
- **Type:** Society recommendations
- **Official URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2019.03.022
- **Access/language:** Paywalled; English
- **Why useful:** Prostate-specific geriatric framework underlying the EAU pathway.
- **Limitations:** Nutrition is one CGA domain, not a nutrition protocol; full text requires authorised access.
- **Decision:** **Secondary RAG** unless licensed full text is available; otherwise **metadata only**.

**Conclusion:** No prostate-specific nutrition guideline exists. ESPEN must supply the actual nutrition-management logic.

## Bladder/urothelial-specific sources

### EAU Guidelines on Muscle-Invasive and Metastatic Bladder Cancer

- **Organisation:** EAU
- **Version:** 2026
- **Scope:** MIBC/metastatic bladder cancer
- **Clinical use:** Fitness for cystectomy, frailty, sarcopenia, albumin as prognostic information, peri-operative pathway
- **Type:** Clinical practice guideline
- **Official URL:** https://uroweb.org/guidelines/muscle-invasive-and-metastatic-bladder-cancer
- **Direct PDF:** https://d56bochluxqnz.cloudfront.net/documents/full-guideline/EAU-Guidelines-on-Muscle-invasive-and-Metastatic-Bladder-Cancer-2026_2026-03-12-154326_cgak.pdf
- **Access/language:** Open; English
- **Why useful:** Current bladder-specific authority. It identifies frailty and sarcopenia as clinically relevant around cystectomy.
- **Limitations:** Not sufficiently detailed to operate a nutrition pathway by itself.
- **Decision:** **Primary RAG**, paired with ESPEN Surgery.

### ERAS Society radical cystectomy recommendations

- **Title:** *Guidelines for Perioperative Care After Radical Cystectomy for Bladder Cancer*
- **Organisation:** ERAS Society
- **Year:** 2013; no formal cystectomy update identified
- **Clinical use:** Pre-operative screening/support, fasting, carbohydrate loading, early oral feeding, EN/PN, ileus prevention
- **Type:** Society guideline
- **Official record:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24189391/
- **Access/language:** Abstract open; official full text paywalled; English
- **Why useful:** Only formal ERAS guideline dedicated to radical cystectomy.
- **Limitations:** Old and partly extrapolated from colorectal surgery. Immunonutrition statements predate newer bladder-specific evidence.
- **Decision:** **Secondary RAG**, always accompanied by EAU 2026 and ESPEN Surgery 2021.

### AFU TVIM 2024-2026

- **Organisation:** Comite de Cancerologie de l'AFU
- **Scope:** Muscle-invasive bladder cancer
- **Clinical use:** French cystectomy/ERAS pathway and urinary-diversion follow-up
- **Type:** National guideline
- **Official URL:** https://www.urofrance.org/recommandation/recommandations-francaises-du-comite-de-cancerologie-de-lafu-actualisation-2024-2026-tumeurs-de-la-vessie-infiltrant-le-muscle-tvim/
- **Access/language:** Current full text access unclear/paywalled; French
- **Why useful:** Best disease-specific French source.
- **Limitations:** Nutrition is embedded rather than comprehensive.
- **Decision:** **Secondary RAG** after authorised full-text acquisition; otherwise **metadata only**.

## General oncology sources

### ESPEN Practical Guideline: Clinical Nutrition in Cancer

- **Organisation:** ESPEN
- **Year:** 2021
- **Scope:** General adult oncology
- **Clinical use:** Screening, assessment, referral, dietary counselling, ONS, EN, PN, surgery, systemic therapy, radiotherapy, cachexia and palliative care
- **Type:** Practical guideline
- **Official PDF:** https://www.espen.org/files/ESPEN-Guidelines/ESPEN-practical-guideline-clinical-nutrition-in-cancer.pdf
- **Access/language:** Open; English
- **Why useful:** Most complete operational source across prostate and bladder treatment phases.
- **Limitations:** No tumour-specific pathways and limited urinary-diversion guidance.
- **Decision:** **Primary RAG and principal nutrition authority**.

### ESPEN Guidelines on Nutrition in Cancer Patients

- **Organisation:** ESPEN
- **Year:** 2017
- **Scope/type:** General oncology, full scientific guideline
- **Direct PDF:** https://lms.espen.org/files/ESPEN-Guidelines/ESPEN_guidelines_on_nutrition_in_cancer_patients.pdf
- **Access:** Open
- **Why useful:** Detailed rationale and evidence underpinning the practical guideline.
- **Limitations:** Older evidence; substantial overlap with 2021.
- **Decision:** **Secondary RAG/evidence parent**, not a competing current pathway.

### ESPEN Practical Guideline: Clinical Nutrition in Surgery

- **Organisation:** ESPEN
- **Year:** 2021
- **Scope:** Major surgery, including cancer surgery
- **Clinical use:** Risk screening, pre-operative optimization, early oral/enteral feeding and PN indications
- **Direct PDF:** https://www.espen.org/files/ESPEN-Guidelines/ESPEN_practical_guideline_Clinical_nutrition_in_surgery.pdf
- **Access/language:** Open; English
- **Decision:** **Primary RAG** for cystectomy and prostatectomy.

### ESMO Cancer Cachexia in Adult Patients

- **Organisation:** ESMO
- **Year:** 2021
- **Scope:** Advanced cancer/cachexia
- **Clinical use:** Cachexia identification, assessment, multimodal intervention and goals-of-care alignment
- **Type:** Clinical practice guideline
- **Official full text:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2059702921000491

### MASCC advanced-cancer nutrition guidance

- **Organisation:** MASCC

- **Clinical use:** Clinically assisted nutrition, specialist-dietitian referral and benefit/burden decisions
- **Type:** Expert guidance

### ASPEN outpatient screening recommendations

- **Title:** *Malnutrition Risk Screening in Adult Oncology Outpatients: An ASPEN Systematic Review and Clinical Recommendations*
- **Year:** 2024
- **Clinical use:** Selecting validated screening tools and referral thresholds
- **Official URL:** https://doi.org/10.1002/jpen.2688
- **Access:** Full text generally subscription-based
- **Decision:** **Secondary RAG if licensed**; otherwise **metadata only**. Questionnaire scoring remains deterministic tooling.

### NCI PDQ Nutrition in Cancer Care

- **Organisation:** US National Cancer Institute
- **Version:** Continuously updated
- **Type:** Health-professional evidence summary, not a graded guideline
- **URL:** https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/side-effects/appetite-loss/nutrition-hp-pdq
- **Access:** Open
- **Use:** Symptoms affecting intake, dietitian role, and oral/enteral/parenteral support.
- **Decision:** **Secondary RAG**.

ASCO cachexia and diet/weight guidelines are useful cross-checks but their full texts are access-restricted. Keep them as **metadata only** unless RCPia has licensed access.

## Required patient-ledger fields

RCPia should capture:

- Current, usual and pre-treatment weight; percentage loss over 1, 3 and 6 months.
- Height and BMI, but never use BMI alone.
- Current intake and duration of reduced intake.
- Appetite and nutrition-impact symptoms: nausea, vomiting, mucositis, dysphagia, chewing problems, taste change, pain, diarrhoea, constipation and early satiety.
- Validated screening result and date: MST/MUST/NRS-2002 or locally selected instrument.
- Dietitian assessment, including PG-SGA where used.
- Muscle mass and function: CT-derived muscle estimate when available, grip strength, gait/mobility and sarcopenia status.
- ECOG/performance status, frailty, G8/CGA status in older patients.
- CRP/inflammatory burden; albumin as prognostic/contextual information, **not a standalone malnutrition diagnosis**.
- Creatinine/eGFR and electrolyte/acid-base abnormalities.
- Treatment phase: surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, immunotherapy, ADT, advanced or end-of-life care.
- Nutrition route, prescribed targets, actual delivery and reassessment date.
- For urinary diversion: diversion type, renal function, bicarbonate/electrolytes and long-term vitamin B12 surveillance plan.

BMI, percentage weight loss and questionnaire totals belong in deterministic tools. Guidelines explaining when to screen, interpret results or refer belong in RAG.

## Remaining gaps

- No dedicated prostate- or bladder-cancer nutrition guideline.
- No current ERAS Society cystectomy update and no prostatectomy-specific ERAS nutrition guideline.
- No authoritative comprehensive nutritional follow-up protocol after urinary diversion, particularly for B12, metabolic acidosis and electrolyte monitoring.
- No bladder-specific nutrition guidance for cisplatin, pelvic radiotherapy or checkpoint inhibitors.
- Limited guideline-level ADT nutrition guidance beyond bone health and general metabolic/sarcopenia management.
- PG-SGA, MNA and other tool licensing/implementation requirements must be checked before embedding forms.

## Authority and conflict rule

Use ESPEN 2021 for nutrition management; EAU/AFU for disease context; ESPEN Surgery plus ERAS for operations; ESMO for cachexia; MASCC for clinically assisted nutrition in advanced disease. More recent bladder-specific trial evidence should override older extrapolated immunonutrition claims.
